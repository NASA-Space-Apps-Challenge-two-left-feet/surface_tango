FEMA EMI IS-0703b states that the National Response Framework priorities of response are to save lives, protect property and the environment, stabilize the incident, and meet basic human needs by stabilizing community lifelines. Surface Tango uses that order as the wording of the response posture. It does not turn the priorities into hectare, population, or dollar triggers.
